॥ सप्तपदी ॥ The Saptapadi: the seven vows

The seven sacred steps

सप्तपदी

Seven rounds around Agni

the eternal witness — and with every round, a promise.

  1. The first vow

    पहला फेरा

    For nourishment

    To provide for and care for each other.

  2. The second vow

    दूसरा फेरा

    For strength

    Of body, mind and spirit, together.

  3. The third vow

    तीसरा फेरा

    For prosperity

    To build a life by honest means.

  4. The fourth vow

    चौथा फेरा

    For happiness

    Through love, respect and trust.

  5. The fifth vow

    पाँचवाँ फेरा

    For family

    To cherish the children and elders in our care.

  6. The sixth vow

    छठा फेरा

    For the seasons

    To walk together through every one of them.

  7. The seventh vow

    सातवाँ फेरा

    For friendship

    To remain companions for life.

सात फेरे · सात वचन

Seven steps. Seven promises.

One lifetime.
